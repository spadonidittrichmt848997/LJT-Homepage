I am Junteng Liu, a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by Professor Junxian He. I completed my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), graduating with a B.Eng. in June 2024; Professor Junxian He also advised me during my undergraduate studies at SJTU. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
* LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
